# Instagram caption — "Apple's rumored camera that doesn't record"

Apple's rumored home camera won't record video. At all. 📷🚫

It's code-named J450, per Bloomberg's Mark Gurman: a small metal cylinder, about the size of an oversized lip balm, with a slow, low-frame-rate eye.

So what does it do? It watches the room, and writes. Someone walks in, you get a note. The dog hops on the couch, you get a note. In Gurman's words: you're not getting anything but text descriptions. 📝

It reportedly recognizes the people who live there, and pets, and tells you when someone comes or goes.

Apple's pitch is privacy: an alternative to mass surveillance, because there's no footage to watch, leak, or stream.

But here's the catch. If someone breaks in, what do you hand the police? A note saying a person entered the kitchen. No clip. No proof for your insurer. 🕵️

It's not alone: reports say Apple and Google are rethinking what "recording" even means, with gadgets that transcribe and summarize without saving the video.

Don't pencil it in yet. Apple hasn't announced this camera, and it's reportedly not due until 2027. The smart home display it pairs with could show up as soon as this month.
