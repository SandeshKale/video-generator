How this schedule was set

  • Reels go out at 11:00 and 20:30 SGT, Monday to Friday, to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts at once. 20:30 SGT is 8:30 am in New York, 1:30 pm in London (12:30 pm after 25 Oct) and 6 pm in India.
  • LinkedIn gets one post a day, not two. A second post within the same day competes with the first for reach. It goes out at 20:30 SGT, the start of the US working morning, when B2B feeds are busiest.
  • Fresh news goes first. Stories from the last few days lead week 1, while they are still news. Evergreen explainers (tokens, offline AI, trading pipeline) take the 20:30 slots, which have the widest reach. Older September stories close the run and carry a "re-check" flag.
  • Fridays are lighter (robot fight, cartwheel), matching your weekend-decompression plan.
  • For each post: pin the first comment from the caption pack, then stay 15 minutes and reply to every comment.
