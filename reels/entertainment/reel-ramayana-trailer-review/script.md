## Unique findings (first-hand, measured, not seen in the coverage I found)
1. **The villain is introduced first and the hero is introduced last.** "Ravana" is spoken at 0:39, "Ram" at 1:13 (34 s gap), and Ram's entrance (about 1:02–1:15) is the **darkest picture (mean luma 42 vs 64.5, −35%)** and the **quietest audio** (1:10–1:20 window, −20.6 LUFS) of the trailer. *Reading (opinion):* the trailer saves its brightest, loudest moments for the antagonist; the hero is a hush.
2. **No actor is named anywhere.** Across 1-fps frames of the whole file and the end poster, the only billed people are Namit Malhotra and Nitesh Tiwari; the film is sold on "The Odyssey and Dune". (Tiny print could hide a name; none found.)
3. *Supporting:* the giant creature frame (1:10) is among the darkest 20 of 156 frames. Opinion: low-key light protects unfinished VFX, which the producer has said.
Claim hygiene: say "I measured / I found", never "nobody has noticed".
