# Avatar prompts: Ramayana trailer review (Hindi voice)

Generate **Part 1 first** and review it before running the rest (same photo for every part: `prototypes/reel-app/public/profile.jpg`). Each prompt is under 4000 characters. **Check first:** that your avatar tool supports Hindi speech. If it only offers English voices, use the Hinglish (Roman) fallback lines at the bottom and keep the same voice instructions.

## Part 1 of 4 — hook and verdict (0–15 s)

```
Create a photorealistic vertical 15-second talking-head video from the attached photo. The delivery is natural, relaxed and warm, like a friend telling you something fun over chai. Not an announcer, not a presenter, not acting.
IDENTITY: match the photo exactly in every frame (face shape, eyes, nose, mouth, jaw, skin texture, hair, beard, glasses, apparent age). No beautifying, slimming or airbrushing. If no clear face is visible, do not generate.
FRAMING: vertical medium close-up from mid-chest up, hands visible near the lower edge when they move, eyes in the upper third, camera locked on a tripod (no zoom, pan or shake). Soft cinematic key light with gentle fill on a black background with depth. Attire: exactly as in the photo, static. Keep framing, light and attire identical across all parts.
PERFORMANCE (keep it subtle):
- Gaze: soft, steady eye contact with the lens; tiny natural eye movements that always return to the lens. Never stare, never look away.
- Face: neutral-warm with a faint natural smile that comes and goes. Eyebrows move only slightly and only with meaning. No wide smiles, no raised-brow acting, no cheek bunching.
- Head and body: nearly still, with natural breathing and micro-movement. At most two small nods in the whole clip, on key words.
- Hands: subtle, slightly restrained gestures that support the words, like a credible expert explaining calmly: two or three small, slow movements per clip, kept low near the chest, then back to rest. Open palm or a gentle 'one point' hand shape only. Natural five-finger hands, no pointing at the camera, no waving, no fast or repeated movements, no hands crossing the face.
- Blinks: natural, roughly every 3-5 seconds, none in the first second.
- Mouth: natural speech movement with moderate opening; do not over-articulate.
VOICE: speak in HINDI (natural spoken Hindi with Indian film-and-tech words as Indians say them, not Sanskritised, not English). Super friendly, soothing yet punchy: warm, grounded, chest-resonant, like a trusted elder-brother friend; sounds close to a good microphone in a quiet room (dry, no echo or reverb). Everyday pace, short punchy sentence endings that land downward, a soft smile in the voice. Natural pitch movement: lighter and slightly higher on a question, lower and a touch slower on the key line, a short pause before the most important line. Small human pauses at commas; at most one lightly stressed word per sentence; slightly softer on asides. No announcer tone, no sing-song, no robotic flatness, no filler sounds.
PRONUNCIATION: 'रामायण' = 'raa-maa-yan', 'ट्रेलर' = 'tray-lar', 'सेकंड' = 'sek-und'
MOOD BY SECTION: 0-3s ENTHUSIASTIC AND ENGAGING: bright, energetic, a confident open smile, a little quicker, still natural (no shouting, no wide-eyed acting, no head bobbing), dead-on at the lens; 3-9s warm and curious, one small open-palm gesture on 'सेकंड दर सेकंड'; 9-14s calm and sure, a single small nod on 'देखने लायक है'.
SCRIPT: "रुकिए! रामायण का नया ट्रेलर आया, और मैंने इसे सेकंड दर सेकंड नापा। एक चीज़ ऐसी मिली, जो मुझे कहीं और नहीं दिखी। पहले सीधी बात: ये ट्रेलर देखने लायक है।"
TIMING: begin speaking at 0.0 s, no lead-in (this is the opening hook). Finish by about 14.2 s, then rest silently with a relaxed closed mouth until 15 s.
AVOID: morphing, identity drift, flicker, jaw drop, teeth distortion, plastic skin, exaggerated expressions, glitchy lip sync, any on-screen text or subtitles.
```
