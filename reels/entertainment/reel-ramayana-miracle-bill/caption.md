# Caption: "VFX ka bill" (Ramayana Part 1 x the cost of computed light)

## Instagram caption
Chandrayaan-3: ₹615 crore. Ramayana Part 1: ₹835–900 crore, reports ke mutabik. Chaand par jaana sasta, chamatkar dikhana mehnga? 👇

Asli duniya mein sooraj ki kiran muft hai. Parde par har kiran ke liye computer ko poochna padta hai: kahan girti hai, kahan mudti hai, kahan rukti hai. Isi sawaal ka bill lagta hai.

Film ke producer Namit Malhotra ki company DNEG ye bill pehle bhi bhar chuki hai: Interstellar ke black hole ke kuch frame reports ke mutabik ~100 ghante tak render hue, aur us kaam par do asli science paper chhape. Ramayana mein cinematographer Pankaj Kumar ke mutabik motion capture floor par qareeb 42 infrared camera the, aur Jatayu poori tarah CG hai; us scene mein sirf Yash aur Sai Pallavi live the.

Poora bill (render ke ghante, bijli) public nahi hai. Budget ke aankde bhi alag hain: Part 1 ke liye 835–900 crore, do hisson ka milakar makers ke mutabik ~4,000 crore. Ye tulna adhoori hai: ek mission ki aur ek film ki. Par sawaal asli hai.
