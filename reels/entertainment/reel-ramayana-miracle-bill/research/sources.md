- "10-head sequence took 6–7 days to shoot" — no source found; the THR India interview does not describe how Ravana's heads are made. Dropped.
- "Sunny Deol as Hanuman / Raghav Juyal as Meghnath" — not needed, unverified here. Dropped.
- Pelagornis wingspan and gold-leaf thickness: reserved for the Jatayu/Lanka follow-ups; not used here.
- "Render hours / electricity for Ramayana" — not public; the reel says so.
