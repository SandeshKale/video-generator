## The story, with numbers (The Week 8 Sep, Gulf News 9 Sep, The Week 20–21 Sep)
- *Hanuman Ansh*: a devotional drama about Neem Karoli Baba, director Vishal Chaturvedi, budget **≈ ₹2 crore**, no stars, no big marketing, opened **7 Aug on 25 screens**, ≈ ₹10 lakh on day 1 ([The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/entertainment/2026/09/08/hanuman-ansh-unexpected-box-office-success.html), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/entertainment/hanuman-ansh-box-office-small-budget-hit-crosses-rs-169-billion-earning-84-times-its-cost-1.500668036)).
- Week 1 ₹1.08 cr (25 screens) → week 2 ≈ ₹40 lakh (**40 screens**) → week 3 ₹10.40 cr (**≈ 400 screens**) → week 4 ≈ **₹61 cr** → week 5 ₹70.25 cr (Gulf News, 2 days short). By day 32: ₹132 cr net / ₹156 cr gross; by day 45 (Sacnilk via [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/entertainment/2026/09/21/hanuman-ansh-box-office-collection-day-45-rs-350-crore-club-entry.html)): **₹272.56 cr net, ₹347 cr worldwide gross** (≈ 170× the budget if the ₹2 cr figure is right). Hombale Films took it overseas on 11 Sep. A later headline cites ₹4.75 cr in week 10.
- Why it spread: word of mouth, families and communities, exhibitors adding shows after full houses.

## The first-hand, derived angle (label "my arithmetic from reported numbers")
**Screens followed the audience, not the other way round.** Per-screen week-4 take ≈ ₹61 cr ÷ ≈ 400 screens ≈ **₹15 lakh per screen**, about **3.5×** the week-1 take of ₹1.08 cr ÷ 25 ≈ **₹4.3 lakh per screen** (screen counts are "near 400"/"25", so approximate). Meanwhile the week-2 dip (40 screens) shows how close it came to being pulled. This links straight to the screens story that is live this week (Drishyam 3's week-3 show demand, Jailer 2 Hindi without national chains): *a screen is the scarce resource, and a ₹2 crore film earned its own.*
Also checked: The Week's "1,168% jump" for week 3 doesn't match its own numbers (₹10.40 cr vs ₹40 lakh is about 26×); use the rupee figures, not the percentage.
