## The story (all reported 14 Oct 2026 unless noted)
- **Drishyam: The Conclusion** (Hindi, Ajay Devgn, Panorama Studios) opened 2 Oct and crossed ₹300 cr on Sun 11 Oct. Its distributor asked cinemas for a **minimum number of daily shows from 16 Oct (week 3)**, by size of cinema: 1 screen: 3 shows · 2 screens: 5 · 3 screens: 6 (incl. a morning show) · 4 screens: 7 · 5 screens: 8 · 6 screens: 10 · 7+ screens: 15 ([Bollywood Hungama](https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/news/bollywood/drishyam-the-conclusion-show-sharing-row-panorama-studios-week-3-show-demands-put-exhibitors-in-a-fix-as-nayyi-navelli-prahaar-jailer-2-gear-up-for-release/), [Box Office Worldwide](https://boxofficeworldwide.com/movies-latest-news/drishyam-the-conclusion-week-3-show-demand-cinemas-scrambling/)).
- **Five new films** compete for the same screens that Friday/week: Nayyi Navelli, Prahaar, Street Fighter, Ranabaali and **Jailer 2** (Tamil, Rajinikanth, Sun Pictures, released 15 Oct). Several cinemas had not opened week-3 bookings while negotiating; a trade source said a settlement could come by Wed or Thu, and "it's difficult to say, going by recent experiences". **Outcome not found as of 16 Oct: re-check before scripting.**
- **Jailer 2's Hindi version ("Rajini: The Jailer 2")** is not getting a national multiplex release (PVR INOX, Cinepolis, Miraj), likely because of a planned OTT premiere about four weeks after release; it still sold **39,485** Hindi tickets for Thu 15 Oct. The film sold **14.15 lakh** day-1 tickets by 3:45 pm on 14 Oct (about **18–19 lakh** for the weekend), projected opening **> ₹30 cr** ([Bollywood Hungama](https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/news/box-office-special-features/jailer-2-advance-booking-explodes-rajinikanth-starrer-sells-14-15-lakh-tickets-for-day-1-hindi-version-surprises-with-nearly-40000-tickets-despite-no-national-multiplex-release/)). ## Why this is a better reel
- **Concrete, India-specific, human stakes:** a Friday plan (what's playing near you) turns on a behind-the-scenes negotiation; viewers meet this as "no shows left for X".
- **Masses and classes:** masses get Drishyam vs Rajinikanth; classes get how distribution works (min-show demands, OTT windows, chain vs single screen).
- **A first-hand angle (derived, labelled):** turn the show ask into a share of a cinema's day. *Assumption, not sourced:* a screen runs ~4–5 shows/day. Then the ask is ≈ 60–75% of a single screen's day (3 of 4–5), ≈ 35–44% of a four-screen multiplex (7 of 16–20) and ≈ 43–54% of a seven-screen one (15 of 28–35). So the smallest cinemas carry the heaviest share. Put "illustrative, assumes 4–5 shows a screen" on the card; check with trade sources before publishing.
- **Fresh, not obvious:** coverage lists the demand table; few explain what it means for one theatre.
