## New technique this reel introduces
Real-data waveform clones: the avatar's own PCM peaks are sampled per frame (pure function of `t`) and duplicated with deterministic detune; plus a Blender rigid-body gavel/stamp driven by baked frames, plus a 275-element deterministic sprite field.
