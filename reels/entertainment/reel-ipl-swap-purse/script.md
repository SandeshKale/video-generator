## One-sentence story

## Mockup findings (18 Nov 2026) — `mockup/mockup.mp4` (16 s, silent), `mockup/contact-sheet.png`
Three scenes from three families: (1) real photo cards (PMO/PIB crops of Arshdeep and Hardik) with rolling ₹ counters, a slammed Devanagari caption with a keyword block, telestrator arrows drawn on the photos and club-colour chips; (2) an instanced 3D stage: 111 leather-textured balls roll in one by one from the right onto a 12×10 board, with a rolling wicket counter, soft key light and shadows; (3) instanced 3D gold coins falling and bouncing into two stacks (33 and 19 coins for ₹32.6 and ₹18.6 crore, last coin scaled to its fraction), seat squares lighting (9 and 6), a dashed ring and an arrow on Punjab's stack, a slow camera rise. Cut into scene 3 on a flash.
- **Render cost:** 480 frames (16 s at 30 fps) in **2 min 18 s** (about 0.29 s per frame) in headless Chromium with SwiftShader, so a 70 s full film is about 10 minutes. Far cheaper than the Cycles route.
- **What works:** everything on screen moves by itself (counters, balls, coins, arrows, seats), real photos carry the news, and each number has a source tag and each photo a credit chip.
- **Honest limits:** the PMO photos show the players in **India team kit with the Prime Minister's hand in frame, from July 2024, not IPL kit**: the film labels this and crops Modi out; the coins read as plain gold cylinders (no embossing yet); the board in scene 2 is dark and could carry the club colours; arrows sit over the photos and need hand-placed anchors in the full build; Preity, Wankhede, Ahmedabad, Iyer and Ponting were not fetched (Wikimedia rate limits; only the auction photo downloaded), so the mockup uses the four PMO images only.
