Hold on! Jailer 2 earned about ₹125 crore worldwide on day one.
But the Hindi version? Only ₹3.5 crore.
Why? Let's look at the numbers.
About 16,600 shows ran on day one. 4,300+ were Hindi: one in four.
And the Hindi opening was 10× the first Jailer's.
Now the money. Hindi was a quarter of the shows, but only 6% of the earnings.
Hindi shows were 20% full. Tamil: about 71%.
That's a big gap.
Average per show: about ₹46,000 in Tamil, ₹8,000 in Hindi.
That's my arithmetic.
So 80% of Hindi seats stayed empty.
But why?
Reportedly, chains wanted an 8-week window. OTT may come in 4.
