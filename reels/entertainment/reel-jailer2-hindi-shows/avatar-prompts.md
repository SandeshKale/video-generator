# Avatar prompts: Jailer 2 Hindi shows (Hindi voice, 5 parts, brisk pace)

Generate **Part 1 first** and review it before running the rest (same photo for every part: `prototypes/reel-app/public/profile.jpg`). Five parts of about 15 s each; speech should end by ~13 s at a brisk pace. Each prompt is under 4000 characters. **Check first:** that your avatar tool supports Hindi speech. If it only offers English voices, use the Hinglish (Roman) fallback lines at the bottom and keep the same voice instructions.

## Part 1 of 5 — hook (0-15 s)

```
Create a photorealistic vertical 15-second talking-head video from the attached photo. The delivery is natural, relaxed and warm, like a friend telling you something fun over chai. Not an announcer, not a presenter, not acting.
IDENTITY: match the photo exactly in every frame (face shape, eyes, nose, mouth, jaw, skin texture, hair, beard, glasses, apparent age). No beautifying, slimming or airbrushing. If no clear face is visible, do not generate.
FRAMING: vertical medium close-up from mid-chest up, hands visible near the lower edge when they move, eyes in the upper third, camera locked on a tripod (no zoom, pan or shake). Soft cinematic key light with gentle fill on a black background with depth. Attire: exactly as in the photo, static. Keep framing, light and attire identical across all parts.
PERFORMANCE (keep it subtle):
- Gaze: soft, steady eye contact with the lens; tiny natural eye movements that always return to the lens. Never stare, never look away.
- Face: neutral-warm with a faint natural smile that comes and goes. Eyebrows move only slightly and only with meaning. No wide smiles, no raised-brow acting, no cheek bunching.
- Head and body: nearly still, with natural breathing and micro-movement. At most two small nods in the whole clip, on key words.
- Hands: subtle, slightly restrained gestures that support the words, like a credible expert explaining calmly: two or three small, slow movements per clip, kept low near the chest, then back to rest. Open palm or a gentle 'one point' hand shape only. Natural five-finger hands, no pointing at the camera, no waving, no fast or repeated movements, no hands crossing the face.
- Blinks: natural, roughly every 3-5 seconds, none in the first second.
- Mouth: natural speech movement with moderate opening; do not over-articulate.
VOICE: speak in HINDI (natural spoken Hindi with Indian film-and-tech words as Indians say them, not Sanskritised, not English). Super friendly, soothing yet punchy: warm, grounded, chest-resonant, like a trusted elder-brother friend; sounds close to a good microphone in a quiet room (dry, no echo or reverb). Make the delivery smooth, flowing and rhythmic: join phrases in one breath with a gentle musical cadence (a short line, then a longer one, then a short one), soft consonants, no clipped stops, no abrupt pitch jumps, like a calm storyteller. Pace: brisk, quick and energetic, about three words a second, tight pauses, no drawn-out words, short punchy sentence endings that land downward, a soft smile in the voice. Natural pitch movement: lighter and slightly higher on a question, lower and a touch slower on the key line, a short pause before the most important line. Small human pauses at commas; at most one lightly stressed word per sentence; slightly softer on asides. No announcer tone, no sing-song, no robotic flatness, no filler sounds.
PRONUNCIATION: 'जेलर टू' = 'jay-lar too', 'वर्ज़न' = 'var-zhan', 'आँकड़ों' = 'aan-kad-on'
MOOD BY SECTION: 0-3s ENTHUSIASTIC AND ENGAGING: bright, energetic, a confident open smile, a little quicker, still natural (no shouting, no wide-eyed acting, no head bobbing), dead-on at the lens; 3-9s quick and punchy, a slight inner-brow raise on 'हिंदी वर्ज़न'; 9-13s curious and brisk, a small lean-in on 'ऐसा क्यों हुआ?'.
SCRIPT: "रुकिए! जेलर टू ने पहले ही दिन दुनिया भर में सवा सौ करोड़ के आसपास कमाए। पर हिंदी वर्ज़न? सिर्फ़ साढ़े तीन करोड़। ऐसा क्यों हुआ? चलिए, आँकड़ों से समझते हैं।"
TIMING: begin speaking at 0.0 s, no lead-in (this is the opening hook). Finish by about 14.2 s at the latest (aim for about 13 s). Speak at a steady, slightly brisk pace so every sentence is fully completed and none is cut off; then rest silently with a relaxed closed mouth until 15 s.
AVOID: morphing, identity drift, flicker, jaw drop, teeth distortion, plastic skin, exaggerated expressions, glitchy lip sync, any on-screen text or subtitles.
```
