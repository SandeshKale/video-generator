## Storytelling gate (docs/STORYTELLING-GUIDE.md)
- Protagonist: the gilli (a 15 cm stick). Obstacle: why does a tiny stick fly sometimes and just slide other times? Reversal: it is not how hard you hit, it is *where* (same force, centre vs end). Showable mechanism: torque as force × distance; then the same "where" rule inside a bat (vibration node). Honest ending: sweet spot varies by bat; the film does not claim gilli-danda makes pros.
- One-sentence test: *"Where you hit matters more than how hard: a gilli flies from its end, and a bat feels no shock about 15 cm from its tip."*
- But/Therefore: kids hit gilli at the end → it flies. BUT hit the middle (same force) → it only slides. THEREFORE distance from the centre makes the spin (torque). BUT a bat is not a rigid stick: it shakes. THEREFORE one spot shakes least (≈150 mm from tip), the hand feels no shock; elsewhere the hand goes numb.
- Open loops: "kuch anjaan naam crorepati banenge" (hook) → closed by the last line (anyone who hits the right spot). Plant: "jagah badal gayi" (s3) → pay-off "sahi jagah par maaro" (s9).
