# Caption: "Sahi jagah par maaro" (gilli-danda × the bat's sweet spot)

## Instagram caption
16 December ko IPL ki nilaami hai, aur kuch anjaan naam crorepati banenge. Unke bat ki physics, aapne gaon ki gali mein pehle hi dekh rakhi hai. 👇

Gilli ko patthar par tirchha rakho, uske kinare par danda maaro, aur wo hawa mein uchhal jaati hai. Beech mein maaro? Wo bas khisakti hai. Taaqat wahi, bas jagah badal gayi. Ise kehte hain torque: taaqat guna doori.

Bat mein bhi yahi "jagah" ka khel hai. Gend bat par kahin bhi lage, bat kaanpta hai, par ek khaas jagah hai jahan kaanpna lagbhag ruk jaata hai: sire se kareeb 15 cm andar (ek naapi hui bat par, Rod Cross, Univ. of Sydney). Yahan gend lage toh haath ko jhatka nahi lagta. Kinare par lagi? Haath sunn.

Gilli ho ya karodon ka bat, raaz ek hi hai: sahi jagah par maaro.

Aapne gilli-danda khela hai? Apne gaon ka naam comment mein likho.

Sources: Rod Cross, "Physics of Cricket" (physics.sydney.edu.au/~cross/cricket.html); gilli-danda rules (Wikipedia, Protoball); SMAT 14 Nov – 6 Dec 2026 (BCCI calendar via Outlook India) and IPL mini-auction 16 Dec 2026, Goa (Outlook India, WION), both reported.
