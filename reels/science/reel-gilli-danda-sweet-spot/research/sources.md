## Dropped from the brief (and why)
- "Sweet spot = centre of percussion": contradicted by Cross (node ≈150 mm is the feel-based spot; COP is near the handle). The film says "kampan lagbhag ruk jaata hai", never "centre of percussion".
- "Gada / Bhima / Hanuman teach the same physics": unsourced mythology-physics link; dropped (no claim we can show).
- "Ancient / 2,500 years / precursor of cricket": Wikipedia says "may date back" with weak citations; never voiced.
- "Scouts are watching SMAT": media framing not opened first-hand; the voice says only that boys are making a name before the auction.
- "Kinare par maaro toh haath sunn, ball weak": the ball-speed part is not sourced (Cross: coefficient of restitution is lower near the tip, ~0.1 vs ~0.3 mid-blade, per his formula) — we only claim the hand shock.
