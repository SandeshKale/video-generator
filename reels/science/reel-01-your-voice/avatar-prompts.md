# Avatar prompts: "You've never heard YOU" (reel #01, 3 parts × ≤ 15 s)

**Why avatar prompts exist for this reel:** the series voice is the creator's AI-avatar clip voice, the same as the trading-pipeline reel (decision D4 in `../plan-first-10.md`, answered "same as trading pipeline"). Only the **audio** is used; the face is not shown in reel #01 (the face-open test starts at reel #05). Run `python3 scripts/check-avatar-prompts.py reels/science/reel-01-your-voice/avatar-prompts.md` before generating.

**Built on `AVATAR-PROMPT-TEMPLATE.md` (rules R1-R15):** same photo, framing and light in every part; eyes on the lens; no on-screen text; subtle hands (R14); dry, grounded voice (R15); the 0-3s hook is enthusiastic (R4, Part 1 only).

**Designed pauses (done in the edit, not by the avatar, except the second one):** about 0.8 s of silence between Part 1 and Part 2 (the "higher or lower?" guess beat), and about 1.4 s after "say anything." inside Part 3 (the muffled try-it moment). The edit trims or extends them to the exact length; the music/audio mix goes muffled in that gap.

| Word | Say it as | Where |
|---|---|---|
| bone | one syllable, rhymes with "phone" | P1, P3 |
| air | "air" (never "eye-er") | P1 |

Captions get the proper forms back in the edit ("2025").

**How to run:** generate Part 1 first and send it. Check the 0-3 s hook (energetic but natural), "bone" (one syllable), hands (five clean fingers), and eyes on the lens. Then run Parts 2 and 3. Whisper checks each clip on arrival.

## Part 1 of 3  (3592 characters, 34 words)

```
Create a photorealistic vertical 15-second talking-head video from the attached photo. The delivery is natural, relaxed and understated, like a friend telling you something surprising over coffee. Not an announcer, not a presenter, not acting.
IDENTITY: match the photo exactly in every frame (face shape, eyes, nose, mouth, jaw, skin texture, hair, beard, glasses, apparent age). No beautifying, slimming or airbrushing. If no clear face is visible, do not generate.
FRAMING: vertical medium close-up from mid-chest up, hands visible near the lower edge when they move, eyes in the upper third, camera locked on a tripod (no zoom, pan or shake). Soft cinematic key light with gentle fill on a black background with depth. Attire: exactly as in the photo, static. Keep framing, light and attire identical across all parts.
PERFORMANCE (keep it subtle):
- Gaze: soft, steady eye contact with the lens; tiny natural eye movements that always return to the lens. Never stare, never look away.
- Face: neutral-warm with a faint natural smile that comes and goes (brighter and more open only in the 0-3s hook). Eyebrows move only slightly and only with meaning. No wide smiles, no raised-brow acting, no cheek bunching.
- Head and body: nearly still, with natural breathing and micro-movement. At most two small nods in the whole clip, on key words.
- Hands: subtle, slightly restrained gestures that support the words, like a credible expert explaining calmly: two or three small, slow movements per clip, kept low near the chest, then back to rest. Open palm or a gentle 'one point' hand shape only. Natural five-finger hands, no pointing at the camera, no waving, no fast or repeated movements, no hands crossing the face.
- Blinks: natural, roughly every 3-5 seconds, none in the first second.
- Mouth: natural speech movement with moderate opening; do not over-articulate.
VOICE: warm, grounded, conversational Indian-English accent with natural chest resonance, at an everyday pace (a touch brighter and quicker only in the 0-3s hook); sounds close to a good microphone in a quiet room (dry, no echo or reverb). Natural pitch movement like real conversation: lighter and slightly higher on a question, lower and a touch slower on the key instruction, a short pause before the most important line, statements ending downward. Small human pauses at commas and between sentences; at most one lightly stressed word per sentence; slightly softer on asides. No announcer tone, no sing-song, no robotic flatness.
PRONUNCIATION: 'bone' = one syllable, rhymes with 'phone'. 'air' = 'air' (not 'eye-er').
MOOD BY SECTION: 0-3s ENTHUSIASTIC AND ENGAGING: bright, energetic, a confident open smile and a slightly quicker pace, like telling a friend something true about them; eyes locked on the lens; still natural (no shouting, no wide-eyed acting, no head bobbing); 3-8s curious and even, one small open-palm gesture on 'air, and bone'; 8-14s slower and lower, a faint knowing smile, one small nod on 'only'.
SCRIPT: "You've never heard your own voice the way everyone else does. Two routes to your ears: air, and bone. Everyone hears the air. Only you get the bone. A recording only catches the air."
TIMING: begin speaking at 0.0 s, no lead-in (this is the opening hook). Finish by about 14.2 s, then rest silently with a relaxed closed mouth until 15 s.
AVOID: morphing, identity drift, flicker, jaw drop, teeth distortion, plastic skin, exaggerated expressions, glitchy lip sync, any on-screen text or subtitles.
```
