Film mein Ravan ki Lanka sone ki hai. Par duniya mein itna sona hai bhi? Humne eent se naapa.

Ghar ki ek eent teen kilo ki hoti hai. Wahi eent sone ki ho, to saintees kilo. Aaj tak zameen se jitna sona nikla hai, sab ikattha karein to sirf ek dibba banta hai: baees metre ka, ek cricket pitch jitna. Us dibbe ko khol ke eentein banayein to lagbhag saath lakh eentein. Ek pakke ghar mein das hazaar eent lagti hain. Yaani poori duniya ka sona: sirf chhe sau ghar. Ek gaon. Lanka nahi.

To kya sone ki Lanka jhooth hai? Nahi. Valmiki ne safed mahal likhe hain, jinke darwaze, khidkiyan aur deewar sone ki hain. Yaani pathar ke upar sone ki patli parat, jaise mithai par chaandi ka vark. Ek gram sona ek chhoti khidki dhak deta hai. Film walon ne bhi yahi kiya: Ravan ka sinhasan fibre ka hai, upar asli sone ka vark.

Aur asli sone ki Lanka? Bharat ke gharon mein lagbhag pachchees hazaar ton sona rakha hai. Duniya ki har nau mein se ek eent, hamari almaari mein.

## First comment (sources and caveats)
Sources: World Gold Council (about 219,891 tonnes of gold mined in all history, end of 2025; Indian household gold about 25,000 tonnes, other estimates 23,000 to 34,600). Valmiki Ramayana, Sundara Kanda sarga 2 and Yuddha Kanda sarga 3 (prose translation at valmikiramayan.net): white buildings with golden doors and windows, golden archways, a golden rampart; no size in numbers. Throne details: art director Ramesh Andhale, as reported by Business Today and Onmanorama (fibre body, 24-carat gold leaf, about Rs 1 crore).
Caveats: the cube (about 22 m), the gold brick (about 37 kg for a 23 × 11 × 7.5 cm brick), the 60 lakh bricks and the 600 houses are our arithmetic; 10,000 bricks per two-room house is an assumption. Gold leaf is about 0.1 micron, so 1 gram covers roughly half a square metre. "1 in 9" is 25,000 ÷ 219,891. Bricks in the pouring shot are drawn larger than life.
