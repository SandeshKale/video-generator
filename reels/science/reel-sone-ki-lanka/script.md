# Sone ki Lanka — "Itna sona hai bhi?" (plan for approval, mockup stage)

## Why this reel
Jatayu (film character → real thing → honest limit → present-day twist) is the account's best reel. Ravana's golden Lanka is one of Part 1's most talked-about looks, and gold is something every household has a feeling for.

## One-sentence story
The film shows a golden Lanka, but all the gold ever mined would build only one village, so the answer is the one Valmiki's own words and the film's own art team point to: a thin skin of gold over stone.

## Beat sheet (target 70–80 s, first fact by 8 s)
1. Film still of the gilded throne. "Film mein Lanka sone ki hai. Par duniya mein itna sona hai bhi?"
2. BUT gold is heavy: one clay brick 3 kg, the same brick in gold 37 kg (balance).
3. THEREFORE count it: all the gold ever mined is one cube, 22 m, beside a cricket pitch and a house.
4. Re-stack that cube as bricks: about 600 houses. One village. Not a Lanka.
5. BUT read Valmiki: white buildings, golden doors, windows, archways, a golden rampart. He never says solid.
6. THEREFORE gold leaf: 1 gram covers half a square metre. The film did the same: fibre throne, 24-carat leaf.
7. Twist: Indian homes hold about 25,000 tonnes. "Asli sone ki Lanka aapki almaari mein hai." Follow ask before the last line.

## Style (Lemo-Opuscar)
- **Chosen: `data-physical`** — real statistics built as countable real objects at exact scale, lit like a product shoot. Fits because the whole story is one number made physical (brick → cube → village).
- Invariants kept: the unit is an object and is stated once ("1 eent = 37 kilo"); scale is computed, never eyeballed; growth is assembly (bricks placed, never a bar that scales).
- Adapted: vertical 1080×1920; a dark warm studio and a real sunrise instead of the style's pale sweep (creator asked for grand); plain 40 px captions; authored camera locked between moves instead of the style's constant creep.
- Runner-up: `teardown`.

## Real textures and images
Poly Haven CC0: red brick, marble, white sandstone (2k colour, normal, roughness), sunrise and studio panoramas. Three official film stills from TMDB (≤ 2.5 s each, unmodified, credited).

## Camera (shot list to author)
Slow arc on the film still → crane down to the brick pedestal → macro dolly along the balance beam → whip to the field → low orbit round the cube with a person for scale → crane up as the cube unstacks into houses → pull-back to near top view → dolly through the gateway as leaf is laid → push in on an almirah.

## Honesty lines
"Our arithmetic" on every derived number; bricks-per-house is an assumption; Lanka's size is not given in Valmiki; the film team never claimed a solid-gold Lanka; gold price left out unless checked on the day.
