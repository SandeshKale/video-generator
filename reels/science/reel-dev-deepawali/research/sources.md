**Honest limit on the take-away:** a full Moon 1.5–2° away washes the Pleiades out. Covering the Moon with a thumb helps under a clear, dark sky; in a city, binoculars or a phone on night mode. The script and the on-screen label say so.

## 3. The Tripura story (read first-hand: Mahabharata, Karna Parva 33–34, K. M. Ganguli translation, via mahabharata-online.github.io)

| # | What the text says | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 19 | Three brothers: Tarakaksha, Kamalaksha, Vidyunmalin. Maya built three cities: "The golden city was set in heaven, the silver city in the welkin, the iron city was set on the Earth" | voice (metals + places) |
| 20 | Boon: "After a 1,000 years then, we will come together… our three cities… will become united into one… That foremost one amongst the gods who will, with one shaft, pierce those three cities united into one" | voice |
| 21 | Indra (Purandara) "failed to pierce those cities" | YouTube cut |
| 22 | The chariot was the Earth; **the Year became the bow**; the arrow was Agni (staff), Soma (head) and Vishnu (point); Brahma drove | YouTube cut ("dhanush ka naam: Saal") |
| 23 | Shiva "waited thinking of the triple city"; when the cities united he shot; the asuras were burnt | voice |
| 24 | **No date, month, full moon or nakshatra is named** in these two sections | voice: "Mahabharat wali katha mein koi tareekh nahi" |

Not verified first-hand, so not voiced: Mount Meru as the bow and Vasuki as the string (later Puranic and temple-sculpture versions; the playbook draft used these), the Pushya-nakshatra condition, "Abhijit muhurta", and which Purana first places the event on Kartik Purnima (Shiva Purana Rudra Samhita, Padma Purana Kartika Mahatmya and Kashi Khanda are cited by blogs without a verse). The reel says "katha kehti hai" and never claims the event happened on a date.

## 4. Publish-day checks
1. Re-run `python3.13 research/moon_check.py` (needs `ephem`) and compare with Stellarium or timeanddate for Varanasi.
2. Open PIB PRID 1677274 and the UP tourism / Varanasi administration release for the 2026 lamp target; change "das lakh se zyada" only if an official number is published.
3. Check the local weather line: the take-away needs a clear sky.
4. Confirm Guru Nanak Jayanti date on the SGPC calendar before naming it in the caption.
