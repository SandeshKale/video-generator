# Caption — "Devta bhi Diwali manate hain. Par yahi raat kyon?" (Shastra ya Science? Ep 01)

## Instagram caption
24 November ki shaam chand ke bagal mein kya dikhega? Ye reel family group mein bhej do, taaki us raat sab chhat par hon. Follow: nerdlogic.co

Diwali ho gayi, par devtaon ki Diwali abhi baaki hai: Dev Deepawali, 24 November. Us raat Kashi ke ghaat das lakh se zyada diyon se jalte hain. Par yahi raat kyon?

Katha kehti hai: teen asur bhaiyon ke teen udte sheher the, sone, chaandi aur lohe ke. Shart thi: hazaar saal mein ek baar teeno ek ho jaayenge, aur tabhi ek hi teer se toot sakte hain. Shiv ne sahi pal ka intezaar kiya, ek teer, aur devtaon ne khushi mein diye jalaye. Ye hai shastra.

Par Mahabharat ki katha (Karna Parva 33–34) mein koi tareekh nahi likhi. Ye raat aasmaan ne chuni. Diwali amavasya thi, chand gayab. Pandrah din mein chand dharti ka aadha chakkar kaat kar poora ho jaata hai: 9 November se 24 November, 15.3 din. Aur mahine ka naam, Kartik, taaron ke ek guchhe se aaya: Krittika, yaani Pleiades, kyunki is purnima ka chand usi ke paas hota hai.

Lekin har saal nahi. Pichhle saal chand Krittika se lagbhag 16 degree door tha. Is saal, 24 November ki shaam, Varanasi se dekhne par 2 degree se bhi kam: bas teen-chaar chand jitni doori. Humne ye PyEphem se khud nikaala hai.

Aap kya karein: 24 ko chhat par ek diya rakhein, phir chand ko angoothe se dhakein. Bagal mein jo guchha dikhe, wahi hai Kartik ka naam. Saaf aasmaan chahiye; shehar mein doorbeen ya phone ka night mode madad karega.

Tumhare ghar mein Kartik Purnima par kya hota hai: diya, Ganga snaan, ya gurudwara? Comment mein batao.

## First comment (sources and caveats)
Sources: Mahabharata, Karna Parva sections 33–34 (K. M. Ganguli translation): three cities of gold, silver and iron, the thousand-year condition, one shaft; the sections name no date, month or nakshatra. Kartik is "derived from the position of the Moon near the Krittika nakshatra on the full moon day" (Wikipedia, Kartika (month)). Lamp count: "more than a million" (Wikipedia; ANI, 22 Sep 2025, 10 lakh planned); post-event reports for 2025 differ (15 to 25 lakh) and we do not give a ghat count because sources say 84 and 88.
Our calculation: PyEphem 4.2.1, full Moon 24 Nov 2026 at 20:23 IST; new Moon 9 Nov 12:32 IST (15.3 days); Moon 1.3° from Alcyone at 18:00 IST and 1.9° at 19:00 from Varanasi (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai within 0.2°); 16° in the evening of 5 Nov 2025. The script and scripts are in the repo.
Caveats: a full Moon washes the cluster out, so cover it with your thumb under a clear dark sky, or use binoculars in a city. The cities, bow and ghats in the film are cut-paper illustrations, not a map or a historic image. Later tellings (Meru as the bow, Vasuki as the string) are not in the sections we read, so we left them out. Guru Nanak Jayanti falls on the same day; confirm the date with your gurdwara.

## Alt text
A vertical paper-cut animation lit from behind, on a deep indigo night. It opens on the lit steps and temple spires of Varanasi's ghats under a rising full Moon, with the title "Devtaon ki Diwali baaki hai". Three flying paper cities, gold, silver and iron, slide into one line and a single ray passes through all three. A paper page from the Mahabharata shows a blank where a date would be. A diagram shows the Moon travelling half an orbit around the Earth in 15 days. A star chart shows the Moon 16 degrees from the Pleiades last year and under 2 degrees this year. The film ends on a rooftop with one clay lamp and a real hand covering the Moon, then the nerdlogic.co profile.
