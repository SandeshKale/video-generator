# Pushpak Viman: anatomy (draft for approval, 13 Nov 2026)
Folder: `reels/science/reel-pushpak-anatomy/` · Hinglish voice-over only · nerdlogic.co · target ~115-120 s (with pauses) · facts in `research/sources.md` (IDs in brackets).

## One-sentence story
The Ramayana film spent two years and 40-50 designs trying to put "some science" into Pushpak, **but** when we rebuild it part by part from Valmiki's own verses, the text gives it gold, crystal windows and ventilation, and no engine at all; **therefore** the "ancient aircraft manual" people quote turns out to be from 1918 and can't fly, **but** Pushpak's real legacy is two things you can measure: a 2,750 km aerial tour of India written into the epic, and an ISRO spaceplane that lands itself.

## Why this reel
Ram Setu worked (myth + real measurement + honest limit). The film released 5 Nov, its Pushpak sequence is the most-discussed scene [F5, F7], and the director himself framed it as a science question [F3].

## Handling rules
Respectful throughout: no jokes about the epic, no "was it real?" mockery. Film facts are attributed ("director ne kaha", "reviews ke mutabiq"). We never show or copy the film's design: our model is built from verses and labelled **OUR 3D · FROM VALMIKI'S WORDS**. Film-vs-Valmiki difference stated as a fact, not a criticism (the film itself invites viewers to "explore original sources" [F7]). Route sites labelled TRADITIONAL. No "always one more seat" (no verse) [V19].

## Style (Lemo index)
- **Chosen:** `teardown` (Exploded Teardown): a product built from its engineering files and taken apart in front of the camera, each part named with its numbers. **Adapted:** our "engineering files" are Valmiki's verses: every part carries its verse number as the bill of materials. The 🎬 flag (needs real CAD) is met by our own model built from the text.
- **Invariants kept:** layers lift on one axis; the camera travels between them; every part named with its source.
- **Runner-up:** `blueprint` (cyanotype line work), used for the IISc "manual" beat as a sheet that won't fly.
- **Look (new):** warm temple-gold + crystal on deep lapis night; vermilion = film, chalk = Valmiki verse tags, sky-teal = real science; fonts to pick (not Bungee/Unbounded/Fredoka used recently).

## Pause plan (creator, 13 Nov: "good amount of pauses, not too long, not too short")
Marks in the voice text: `/` = short breath **0.3 s** (commas, list items), `//` = comprehension pause **0.6-0.8 s** (after each number, each verse fact, each reveal), a beat change = **0.9-1.0 s** (never longer than 1.2 s).
- Every number gets a `//` after it, so it lands before the next line: 40-50, 2 saal, 6.5 saal, 19 tonne, 1918-23, 1974, 2,750 km, 2024.
- In the parts list (beat 3) each part is its own short sentence with `/`, and the picture adds that part on its word, so voice and picture pause together.
- Questions ("Engine?", "Science ke hisaab se?") get a `//` (≈0.9 s) so the viewer can think.
- Delivery: speed ~0.92 (calm, not slow), one take per breath group, then `scripts/pause_inject.py` with the per-group `pause_after` values from these marks.
- **Gate:** `scripts/voice_pause_report.py` must show silence **28-34 %**, a stop ≥ 0.4 s every **2.5-3.8 s**, no internal pause > 1.2 s, speech rate 2.4-3.0 words/s. Report the numbers with the voice.
