Mai 2026 mein Papua New Guinea ke paas samundar ke neeche ek jwalamukhi phata; NASA ke Landsat 8 ne 4 June ko plume aur badla hua paani dekha, aur June ki shuruaat mein pumice ki parten Admiralty dweepon ke kinaron tak pahunchi. NASA ke mutabik aise raft mahino se saalon tak tair sakte hain; 2012 ka ek raft shuru mein lagbhag 400 vargkilometre ka tha.
