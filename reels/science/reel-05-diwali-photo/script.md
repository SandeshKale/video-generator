## Story line (guide §2.1)
- **Protagonist:** the photo your uncle forwarded (the viewer's own phone).
- **Want:** to show India glowing on Diwali night.
- **Obstacle:** the caption is wrong: it is a 2003 composite; the colours are *years*.
- **Reversal:** the red people call fireworks is **new city lights from 2003**; and a real Diwali-night image from space looks like any night.
- **Honest ending:** NASA says the extra Diwali light is "extremely difficult to detect from space"; what a satellite *would* see is not claimed.
- **Carried question (second ~7):** "So what is this, and what does a real Diwali night look like from space?"
- **One-sentence story test:** *The glowing-India photo everyone forwards looks like Diwali fireworks, but it is a 2003 stack of city lights where the colours are years, therefore the red is new cities, not crackers, but a real satellite image from Diwali week looks like any other night, because one satellite pixel swallows a whole neighbourhood and a diya is a drop in it.*
