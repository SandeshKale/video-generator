## Beat sheet (But / Therefore) and open loops
1. Hook: light on the forehead, no bulb, no wire. **Loop A: how?**
2. **But** the sanctum is under three floors of stone and the noon Sun is high overhead.
3. **Therefore** a periscope: mirror on top, brass pipe, lenses. **Loop A closed.**
4. **But** next year the same setting misses. **Loop B: why?**
5. Because Ram Navami follows the Moon; the Sun is lower or higher each year.
6. **Therefore** astronomers computed 19 years; one mirror, 19 settings, turned by hand. **Loop B closed.**
7. **But** the Earth keeps turning: only a few minutes. And a cloud ends it.
8. Ending: no electricity, only mirrors, arithmetic and a clear sky. Callback to the roof-hole sunbeam.
**One-sentence test:** a sunbeam must reach a forehead under three floors of stone on a date that moves every year, so scientists built a hand-set brass periscope with 19 positions.
