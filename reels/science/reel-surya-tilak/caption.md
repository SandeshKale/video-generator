# Caption — "Sooraj ko andar kaise laaye?" (Surya Tilak)

## Instagram caption
Share kijiye, aur follow karein: nerdlogic.co

Ram Navami ki dopahar, Ayodhya ke mandir mein, sooraj ki ek kiran Ram Lalla ke maathe par tilak banati hai. Na bulb, na taar. To roshni teen manzil pathar ke neeche pahunchti kaise hai?

Jawaab ek periscope hai. Chhat par ek aaina dhoop pakadta hai, peetal ke pipe mein lens use sikodte hain, aur aakhri aaina use sirf 58 mm ke ek bindu par rakh deta hai. Sabse upar ek sheesha garmi rok leta hai.

Asli pareshani yeh hai ki Ram Navami chaand se chalti hai, isliye har saal alag taareekh par aati hai, aur us din sooraj ki oonchai bhi alag hoti hai. Vaigyanikon ne 19 saal ka hisaab lagaya, aur pehla aaina saal mein ek baar haath se ghumaya jaata hai. Baadal ho, to tilak nahi.

Design: CSIR-CBRI Roorkee. Optics aur sooraj ka hisaab: Indian Institute of Astrophysics, Bengaluru. Banaya: Optica, Bengaluru.

Is reel mein mandir ka model ek schematic hai, asli naksha nahi. Murti nahi dikhayi gayi hai. Log aur setting wheel sirf samjhane ke liye hain. Sooraj ki oonchai, 19 saal ki taareekhein aur "2 minute" wali baat hamara apna hisaab hai.

## First comment (sources and caveats)
Sources: PIB release "Indian Institute of Astrophysics plays crucial role in bringing sunlight on forehead of Sri Ram Lalla" (Apr 2024, PRID 2018141); CSIR-CBRI scientists as quoted by PTI, ETV Bharat and Business Today (Apr 2024: 58 mm, brass pipes, heat filter, 4 mirrors and 4 lenses in the final design); Science India magazine (no electricity, yearly manual shift, 19-year cycle).
Caveats: the periscope's real dimensions and layout are not published, so ours is a schematic. Reports differ on how long the tilak lasts (about 3 to 9 minutes), so the reel says "a few minutes". The 19 dates shown are our approximation (new moon + ninth day); the first three match 17 Apr 2024, 6 Apr 2025 and 27 Mar 2026.
