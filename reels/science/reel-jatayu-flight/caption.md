# Caption — "Itna bada pakshi ud sakta hai?" (Jatayu)

## Instagram caption

Ramayana film mein Jatayu ne Ravan ka rath tod diya. Par itna bada pakshi ud bhi sakta hai? Valmiki ne Jatayu ko gidhraj kaha hai, gidhon ka raja. To humne ek asli gidh ko khol ke dekha.

Pankh dhai metre, vazan sirf chhe kilo. Har pankh mein hazaaron baareek kaante hain jo zip ki tarah jud jaate hain. Seene ki maaspeshi poore vazan ka lagbhag ek chauthai hai. Haddiyan andar se khokhli hain, pul ke girder jaisi, aur poora dhaancha vazan ka sirf 5%. Phephdon ke saath nau hawa ki thailiyan. Isiliye ek gidh 11 km upar tak mila hai, Everest se bhi ooncha.

Ab ise bada kijiye. Lambai dugni, to pankh chaar guna, par vazan aath guna. Aaj ka sabse bhaari udne wala pakshi bas 19 kilo ka hai. Itihaas ka sabse bhaari, Argentavis, lagbhag 70 kilo aur 6 metre pankh. Film jitna bada? Vigyan kehta hai, mushkil.

Par asli dukh yeh hai: Bharat ke gidh 10-15 saal mein lagbhag 97% khatm ho gaye. Kisi Ravan ne nahi maara, maveshiyon ki ek dard ki dawa, diclofenac, ne. 2006 mein us par rok lagi. Haryana ke Pinjore mein gidh paale aur chhode ja rahe hain. Us kendra ka naam? Jatayu.

Is reel mein har pakshi, pankh, dhaancha aur aasmaan asli tasveer hai. Maaspeshi aur hawa ki thailiyan humne banayi hain (screen par likha hai). Argentavis ka chitr ek kalakaar ka andaaza hai. Sankhyaein andaaze hain.

Sources: Valmiki Ramayana, Aranya Kanda sargas 50-51 (Jatayu as king of vultures; his size is given only by comparison). Indian vulture size and weight, bird skeleton and flight-muscle shares, nine air sacs: standard ornithology references and Wikipedia summaries. Argentavis: about 70 kg, wingspan estimates 5 to 7.5 m. Vulture decline and diclofenac: Oaks et al. 2004 (Nature), Prakash et al.; India's veterinary ban, 2006. Jatayu Conservation Breeding Centre, Pinjore (BNHS and Haryana Forest Department).
Caveats: "97%" is the reported fall of the three Gyps species over 10 to 15 years; the film's Jatayu has no official size, so we only say "that big is hard"; the x4 / x8 scaling is our own arithmetic; the flight muscle and air sacs are drawn by us on a real museum skeleton (a bearded vulture, Univ. of Pisa); the 100 circling birds are two real photos repeated.
