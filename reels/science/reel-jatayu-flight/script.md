## Beat sheet (But / Therefore) and open loops
1. Hook: the film's Jatayu smashes a chariot. **Loop A: could a bird that big fly?** (viewer asked to guess)
2. **But** Valmiki calls him king of vultures, and real vultures do fly: 2.5 m wings, only 6 kg.
3. **Therefore** open one up: feathers, flight muscle, hollow bones, air sacs. That is how 6 kg stays up.
4. **But** scale it up: double the length, wings x4, weight x8.
5. **Therefore** the heaviest flier today is ~19 kg, the heaviest ever ~70 kg with 6 m wings. **Loop A closed:** film-size, by physics, no; the story's Jatayu is big in courage.
6. **But** the real Jatayus nearly died out: 97% gone, killed by a cattle painkiller. **Loop B: who saves them?**
7. **Therefore** the ban (2006) and a breeding centre named Jatayu. **Loop B closed.**
8. Ending: the film's Jatayu fought for Sita; the real one works for us. Callback to the first image.
**One-sentence test:** a film hero makes us ask how big a bird can fly, the answer hides inside a 6 kg vulture, and that same real vulture needs saving.
