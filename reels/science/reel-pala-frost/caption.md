Din mein wo dhoop se garmi leta hai, raat ko wahi garmi aasmaan ki taraf chhod deta hai. Baadal ho to garmi lautti hai, saaf aasmaan mein nahi. ## First comment (sources and caveats)
Sources: FAO, "Frost Protection: fundamentals, practice and economics", vol. 1, ch. 8 (cold air flows downhill and pools in low spots; covers add about 1–5 °C; irrigation before a frost can give 3–4 °C; "smoke does not help and it does pollute"). Down To Earth, 4 Feb 2024 (Bihar cold and fog spell, preliminary losses: late-sown potato 25–40% vs 15% early-sown, reported by KVK Aurangabad; the article blames cold and fog together, not frost alone). Krishak Jagat, 19 Dec 2024 (Rajasthan Agriculture Department advice: evening irrigation, covers, light smoke). IMD glossary (ground frost vs air frost). Kisan Call Centre 1800-180-1551 (ICAR).
Caveats: the temperatures on screen (4°C air, a colder leaf) are ILLUSTRATIVE, not measurements; the leaf-versus-air gap changes with the night and no single number is claimed. The smoke point is where Indian state advice and FAO disagree; we only say FAO's research did not find a benefit. The field, crop and thermometers are 3D renders, not a real farm.
