# DM for everyone who comments "SAFE"

Hey! Here's the family safe-word checklist 👇

**Set it up today (5 minutes):**
1. Pick a word or short phrase that's easy to remember but impossible to guess: not a pet's name, birthday or anything on social media.
2. Share it **in person** (or on a call you started yourself), never in a group chat or a post.
3. Tell your parents and grandparents what to do with it, slowly, and make them say it back.

**If a call or voice note asks for money urgently:**
1. Don't send anything while on the call, even if the voice sounds exactly right.
2. Hang up. Call back on the number already saved in your phone.
3. Ask for the safe word. No word = no money.

**If money has already gone:** call **1930** immediately (India's cyber fraud helpline, 24×7) and file a complaint at **cybercrime.gov.in**. The faster you report, the better the chance of freezing the transfer.

Source for the safe-word advice: FBI public service announcement, 3 Dec 2024 (ic3.gov).
