India ka apna GPS (NavIC) aapki jagah tab hi bata sakta hai jab 4 satellite chalein. Aaj 3 hain. Kyun? Kyunki har satellite ke andar ek atomic clock hoti hai, aur ek microsecond ki ghadi ki galti = 300 metre ki galti.
