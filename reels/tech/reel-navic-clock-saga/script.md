## Story line (guide §2.1)
- **Protagonist:** India's own GPS (NavIC), shown as the viewer's own phone dot on a map.
- **Want:** a position fix no other country can switch off.
- **Obstacle:** the atomic clocks inside the satellites keep failing; the rescue satellite is stranded.
- **Carried question (second 8):** "Why four? And where did the others go?" (deepened to "how does a clock kill a GPS?" at beat 6.)
- **One-sentence story test:** *India built its own GPS so it would never depend on anyone, but the atomic clocks inside kept failing, therefore the system fell to three working satellites when four are needed, so ISRO built its own clock and on 30 October launched it, but it still has to reach its slot.*
