## 0. Why this topic (and what lost)
Everyone has an unknown number story, so it is the most shareable ("send to the friend who never picks up"). It has fresh, checkable news (TRAI amendment of 18 Sep 2026; Truecaller vs TRAI since July) and a real reversal. Runners-up: **WhatsApp SIM-binding** (relatable, but the 6-hour logout was dropped and reports disagree on what replaced it: unsafe to roast); **"cancel subscription = 7 clicks"** (CCPA dark patterns; good, but company-specific claims need a case file).

## 1. Story line
- **Protagonist:** the unknown number that rings at 2:47 pm (mascot: a smug phone, "UNKNOWN").
- **Want:** you want one uninterrupted afternoon. **Obstacle:** 4,168 crore spam calls a year.
- **Turn (reversal):** the government made telemarketers use *official* number series (1400 / 1600) so you could trust them, and users ignore 81% and 79% of exactly those calls: the "official" label became the new spam. And the apps can't even tag them as spam.
- **Honest ending:** TRAI's 18 Sep fix (apps must feed reports to telcos, undeclared robocalls = spam, up to 5 paise/min) is new; nobody can say yet whether it works.
- **One-sentence test:** *We wanted to be left alone, but 41.7 billion calls got through, therefore the rules gave telemarketers an official street that nobody answers, so the fix is to make the machines declare themselves, and whether you finally get your afternoon back is still unknown.*
