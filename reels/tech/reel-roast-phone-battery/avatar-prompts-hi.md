## Part 1 of 4 — hook: the pillow and the slow cooking (0–15 s)

```
Create a photorealistic vertical 15-second talking-head video from the attached photo. The delivery is lively, playful and teasing, like a friend roasting you over chai, then helping. Not an announcer, not a presenter, not cartoonish.
IDENTITY: match the photo exactly in every frame (face shape, eyes, nose, mouth, jaw, skin texture, hair, beard, glasses, apparent age). No beautifying, slimming or airbrushing. If no clear face is visible, do not generate.
FRAMING: vertical medium close-up from mid-chest up, hands visible near the lower edge when they move, eyes in the upper third, camera locked on a tripod (no zoom, pan or shake). Soft cinematic key light with gentle fill on a black background with depth. Attire: exactly as in the photo, static. Keep framing, light and attire identical across all parts.
PERFORMANCE (expressive and playful, never over the top):
- Gaze: warm, steady eye contact with the lens; tiny natural eye movements that always return to the lens. Never stare, never look away.
- Face: expressive and playfully sarcastic: amused eyes, a knowing half-smirk on the punchlines, a quick one-sided eyebrow lift on a punchline, then back to a warm neutral. No mugging, no cartoonish faces, no wide-eyed acting, no cheek bunching.
- Head and body: relaxed and alive, small natural head tilts with the jokes. At most two small nods in the whole clip, on key words.
- Hands: subtle, slightly restrained gestures: two or three small, slow movements per clip, low near the chest, then back to rest. Open palm or a gentle 'one point' hand shape only. Natural five-finger hands, no pointing at the camera, no waving, no counting on fingers, no fast or repeated movements.
- Blinks: natural, roughly every 3-5 seconds, none in the first second.
- Mouth: natural speech movement with moderate opening; do not over-articulate.
VOICE: speak in HINDI (everyday spoken Hindi; English words like charge, percent, limit, gaming, cover said as Indians say them). Warm, grounded, chest-resonant and teasing; sounds close to a good microphone in a quiet room (dry, no echo or reverb). Natural pitch movement: lighter and higher on a question, lower and a touch slower on the numbers, a short pause just before each punchline, which lands with a little extra bite, never shouted. Slightly brisk pace, statements ending downward. Small human pauses at commas; at most one lightly stressed word per sentence. No announcer tone, no sing-song, no robotic flatness, no filler sounds.
PRONUNCIATION: 'तकिये' = 'ta-kee-ye', 'आँच' = 'aanch', 'दुश्मन' = 'dush-man'
MOOD BY SECTION: 0-3s ENTHUSIASTIC AND ENGAGING: energetic, a confident teasing grin, a little quicker, a small lean-in, still natural (no shouting, no wide-eyed acting, no head bobbing), dead-on at the lens; 3-7s sarcastic delight, a half-smirk on 'बधाई हो'; 7-12s matter-of-fact, one gentle one-point hand shape on 'दो दुश्मन'.
SCRIPT: "फ़ोन रात भर तकिये के नीचे चार्ज करते हो? बधाई हो, बैटरी को धीमी आँच पर पका रहे हो। गर्मी और फुल चार्ज: बैटरी के दो दुश्मन।"
TIMING: begin speaking at 0.0 s, no lead-in. Finish by about 14.2 s at the latest (aim for about 12.5 s), every sentence fully completed; then rest silently with a relaxed closed mouth until 15 s.
AVOID: morphing, identity drift, flicker, jaw drop, teeth distortion, plastic skin, exaggerated expressions, glitchy lip sync, any on-screen text or subtitles.
```
