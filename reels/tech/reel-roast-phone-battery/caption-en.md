# Caption: Roast of Your Phone Battery, English version (Roasted & Explained #01)

## Instagram caption
Send this to the friend who charges their phone under the pillow 🔥📱 Phone battery health, explained (with a roast).

Heat and a full charge are your battery's two worst enemies. Sitting at 100% for hours is the "buffet" problem; add gaming while charging, a thick case and a pillow, and the phone is basically an oven. In Battery University's lab storage table, a year at 40 °C and 100% charge left about 65% capacity; at 25 °C and 40%, about 96%.

The fix: iPhone 15 and later → Settings › Battery › Charging, set the limit to 80%. Older iPhones → Optimized Battery Charging. Many Androids have a "Protect battery"-style setting (names and limits vary). Keep it cool: Apple says 16–22 °C is ideal and above 35 °C can permanently damage capacity. And Apple's own safety guide says don't charge it under a pillow.

The catch: the 65% / 96% numbers are a one-year storage test, not a promise for your exact phone; real-life wear depends on heat, charge level and time.
