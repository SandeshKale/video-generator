Workers at Hyundai just went on strike. Partly over a robot. 🤖

Here's the story, with the numbers:

🦾 The robot is Boston Dynamics' Atlas: 6'2", 56 joints, lifts 50 kg (30 kg sustained).

🏭 Hyundai plans 25,000+ of them across its Hyundai and Kia plants. First wave: the Georgia Metaplant in 2028, Kia Georgia in 2029.

💲 There's no official price. Analysts estimate roughly $130–140K for early units. Hyundai's target is around $30K once 50,000 are built.

✊ In July, the union (~40,000 members) voted on a strike, and more than 34,000 voted yes. Their demand: no humanoid robot enters a Korean factory without the union's approval. Reports call it the first strike tied to humanoid robots.

🚗 Three days of partial walkouts at Ulsan (July 13–15) reportedly disrupted about 5,000 vehicles, roughly ₩200B (~$145M) of output.

🧑‍🏭 Hyundai's answer: workers will move into training, supervising and maintaining the robots.

🤝 The settlement (ratified Aug 31, 61.55% yes): a ₩100,000 base-pay rise, a bonus of 400%+ of monthly pay, retirement age up to 65 (pending legal changes), 500 new technical jobs, and a promise to share robot plans with the union.

📍 For now Atlas isn't headed to Korea. First stop is Georgia, where the plant isn't unionized.
