## Hook

## Voice-over (full copy)

Workers at Hyundai just went on strike. Partly over a robot.
Meet Atlas. Six foot two. Fifty-six joints. It can lift fifty kilos.
Hyundai plans twenty-five thousand of them in its factories. Georgia first. Twenty twenty-eight.
The price? Analysts say about a hundred and thirty thousand dollars each. Hyundai wants it near thirty, once fifty thousand are built.
In July, Hyundai's union voted on a strike. Forty thousand members. Over thirty-four thousand said yes.
Their demand? No humanoid robot enters a Korean factory without the union's approval.
Three days of walkouts disrupted about five thousand cars.
Hyundai's answer? Workers will train the robots. Supervise them. Maintain them.
Last month, they settled. A raise. Retirement at sixty-five. Five hundred new technical jobs. And a promise to share robot plans.
But Atlas isn't headed to Korea yet. First stop is Georgia, where the plant isn't unionized.
Would you work next to one? Follow for what happens next.
