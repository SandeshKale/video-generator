# Caption: "These words give your AI writing away" (Use It Today)

## Caption
Comment REAL and I'll DM you the full list of AI-sounding words + the fix prompt 👇

Words like "delve", "tapestry" and "testament" show up in AI-written text far more than in normal writing. Researchers saw "delves" shoot up in science papers after ChatGPT arrived. Three more tells: "It's not just this, it's that", everything in threes, and endings that say nothing.

The fix: paste this after any AI draft, "Rewrite it like a busy human. Plain words. No lists of three. No big finish."

Honest note: none of these signs proves AI wrote something. They just make writing sound generic.
