# "Billionaire ka right swipe" — the fake Elon tweet and the real app behind it (sarcastic Hinglish explainer, draft v1, 6 Nov 2026)
Status: **research + script + shot list for approval (nothing built).** Method: `docs/STORYTELLING-GUIDE.md`, `docs/STYLE-ROAST-EXPLAINER.md` (roast the concept, truth tests, dark dial 2 with one moment at 3), `docs/QUALITY-FLOOR.md`, authored cinematic camera, "expert sketcher" rule.

## 0. READ FIRST: the premise is false, so the reel is built around that
The creator's brief says *Elon Musk tweeted* this. **He did not** (Lead Stories "Never Posted", 3 Nov 2026; his account has no "bored billionaires" post). The viral claim started as a Facebook post on 1 Nov; the idea itself came from an ordinary X user on 22 Oct and is only a joke/thought experiment. A reel that says "Elon tweeted" would be spreading a fact-checked hoax on a channel that publishes tier-A facts. So this script keeps the creator's idea intact (the full sarcastic breakdown of the app, in four lenses) and makes the correction the **reversal**: *fake tweet, real nerve*. If the creator wants the Musk-tweet framing anyway, we would need a source that does not exist.

## 1. Benchmark to beat (previous = `reel-ray-tracing-backwards`)
Ray tracing: one clean premium engine, 11 scenes, mostly instrument-style diagrams, no crowds, no comedy objects. This reel must beat it by:
1. **A comedy world, not a diagram:** a parody dating-app *world* built as physical 3D objects (a giant phone with a real live-textured screen, a deck of bill cards with physical swipe physics, a gold throne, a rubber NEVER POSTED stamp) lit with the premium pass (HDR studio, DoF, bloom).
2. **Instanced voxel crowds** for scale jokes (3,428 gold-hatted billionaires vs millions of bills), a first for the premium engine.
3. **Swipe physics:** every card flick, fly-off, stamp slam and coin drop is a pure function of `t` (analytic arcs and spring settles), so a swipe actually feels like a swipe.
4. **Four lenses, four sets** (technology, money, morality, ethics), each a different 3D machine (bill conveyor with fraud gate, fee ladder + tax gates, judgement deck, privacy/bias lens), so no scene is "a card with a sentence".
5. Lints at 0 (label-lint, overlay-lint), cold-read of the jokes through the four truth tests.

## 2. Story line (guide 2.1)
- **Protagonist:** one overdue electricity bill (mascot: a googly-eyed paper bill called BILL, 1 month overdue).
- **Want:** to get right-swiped by a bored billionaire.
- **Obstacle:** the app has to be real: fake bills, double payments, fees, tax, who gets chosen, and who sees your address.
- **Carried question (first 8 s):** "Kya bored billionaires ke liye ek 'bill-swipe' app sach me ban sakta hai?"
- **Reversal:** the tweet that started it all was never posted: **fake tweet, real nerve**.
- **Honest ending:** a few small prototypes exist (one is a demo, one pays billers directly); paying a biller directly beats handing cash; the app is a symptom: the bills are real, the swipe is the part that is a joke. No claim that billionaires *should* pay.
- **One-sentence test:** *We wanted to see whether a billionaire bill-swipe app could work, but Elon never tweeted it and the real version hits fraud, double swipes, fees, tax and a deservingness lottery, therefore we broke it down four ways, so we can say the part that works is paying the biller directly, and the part that doesn't is turning someone's rent into a dating profile.*
