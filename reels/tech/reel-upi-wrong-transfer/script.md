# Script: "Sent money to the wrong number?" (UPI wrong-transfer recovery) — 60 s, Hindi voice only, English on screen, FAST (R17), **cinematic**

One sentence: *you sent ₹5,000 to the wrong person; UPI can't cancel it, but there is a six-step ladder (receipt, recipient, app, bank, NPCI, RBI) and the 12-digit RRN is the key that opens every step.* Goal: reach + saves (a save-worthy rescue plan) + shares ("send to the person who always pays the wrong ID").

## Benchmark to beat
Ceiling: `reel-upi-chai-journey` v2 (grid, framed stage, continuous world) and `reel-04-shot-right-now`. New here, the **cinematic grammar**: (1) **real photographic plates, not drawn objects**: people-free photoreal night-rain street, a phone on a wet table, a bank counter, an ATM glow, generated with RealVisXL and given depth with Depth-Anything; (2) **one continuous dolly through layered plates** (WebGL depth parallax with a physically-motivated camera: slow push, rack focus, handheld micro-drift, anamorphic horizontal flare, bloom, halation, film grain, teal-orange grade, vignette); (3) **a motion-graphics layer that lives inside the picture**: the 12-digit RRN is projected onto the wet street as light reflections and later becomes the glowing key that unlocks each chapter; (4) **sound design as a film**: rain bed, sub-hits, a breathing score, silence before the warning. Cinematic ≠ more effects: fewer, bigger, slower shots; low-key light; motivated colour.

## Cinematic rules for this reel (apply to every shot)
- **Shot language:** 9:16 full-bleed with a subtle 2.39 "scope" feel through top/bottom vignettes (not black bars: bars waste 9:16). Each beat is **one slow camera move (dolly in, rack focus, crane down)**, never a static hold. Camera eases with anticipation and settle.
- **Light:** motivated only: phone-screen glow (cool), sodium streetlight (warm), neon reflections on wet ground (accent). Subject separation by rim light. Colour script: **desaturated teal-black monsoon night** (ground) + **warm sodium amber** (the money) + **cold phone cyan** (the app) + **one red** (the wrong transfer). Different from the chai reel's indigo/amber/mint.
- **Depth:** every plate has foreground, midground and background (rain streaks and bokeh in front; far lights soft). Depth-of-field changes with a rack focus on the key object at each beat.
- **Texture:** 35 mm grain (seeded), halation on highlights, slight chromatic aberration at the frame edges, 24-fps feel for the plate motion while overlays are crisp.
- **Type:** minimal and filmic: a **chapter title** in Instrument Serif italic that fades in under the picture (`I · THE RECEIPT`), tiny mono labels, ≤ 3 words of English per overlay; no panels over the picture except the caption strip. Hierarchy still obeys the fixed grid (tracker top, title in the upper third, picture full-bleed, caption + host bottom).
- **Host:** chest-up in the lower-right, graded to the same teal-orange with a warm rim; appears at the chapter turns, never covers the key object.
