# Avatar prompts — "Wi-Fi off. AI on." (5 parts × ≤15 s, new subtler style)
**What changed vs the previous sets (and why):**
- **Shorter and calmer** (~2.4k characters instead of ~3.9k): fewer micro-directives means fewer things for the model to over-perform.
- **Tone first:** the prompt now opens with "friend over coffee, not an announcer"; the old "smart look / smart eyes / alert" wording that produced a stare is gone.
- **Expression budget:** one light mood note per section instead of timed brow/smile commands; explicit limits (faint smile, at most two nods, blinks every 3-5 s, none in the first second, no hand gestures).
- **Voice:** conversational pace, at most one lightly stressed word per sentence, no pitch-rise/drop rules (those made the delivery sing-song).
- **No subtitles:** the tool burned captions into the earlier clips; the prompt now forbids on-screen text (I still crop the host window as a safety net).
- **Identity lock kept but compressed.**

**How to run:** same photo for all five parts. **Generate Part 1 first and look at it** (does it feel like talking to a friend? is the gaze steady?). If yes, run the rest; if it still feels performed, tell me what and I'll tighten once more before you spend credits on the other four.

**Pronunciation to check:** Ollama (oh-LAH-muh), G-P-T O-S-S, Gemma, E four B, PowerShell. If any is off, tell me and I'll give a phonetic respelling.
