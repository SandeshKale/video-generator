Google's SynthID Detector is now open to anyone, and it can sometimes tell you whether an image, video or audio file was made with AI. Here's how it works, and where it stops.

1️⃣ OPEN synthid.com. You sign in first (with Google, Apple or ChatGPT).

2️⃣ UPLOAD an image, video or audio file. (Reported: it's free, with roughly 10 checks a day.)

3️⃣ READ the result. The tool hunts for a hidden SynthID watermark from Google or its partners (OpenAI, NVIDIA and Kakao; Apple is reported to be coming). If it finds one, that's a strong clue the file was made with AI.

The catch ⚠️ No watermark found does NOT mean it's real. A fake made with any other tool, one that doesn't use SynthID, slips straight through. So treat it as one clue, not a verdict. Ask who posted it first, whether the story adds up, and whether a second source confirms it.
