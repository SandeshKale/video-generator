Hey! Here's the checklist from the reel 👇

**Before you forward a viral image, video or audio clip:**

1. **Find where it started.** Who posted it first? A clip with no original source, or only screenshots of screenshots, is a warning sign.
2. **Search a frame.** Take a screenshot of a key frame and run a reverse image search (Google Lens or TinEye). Old footage reused as "breaking news" shows up fast.
3. **Run it through SynthID Detector** (synthid.com; reportedly needs a sign-in). It only reads watermarks from Google and its partners (OpenAI, NVIDIA, Kakao; Apple is coming). If it finds one, that's a strong clue the file was made with AI. If it finds nothing, that proves nothing: the file could be real, or made with a tool that doesn't use SynthID.
4. **Check the details.** Does the place, date, weather, language and clothing match the story? Do hands, text on signs and reflections look right?
5. **Look for a second source.** Has a news outlet or a person you trust confirmed it independently?
6. **When in doubt, don't forward.** A few minutes of checking beats spreading something fake.

One clue is not a verdict: use several.
