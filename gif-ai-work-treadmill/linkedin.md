# LinkedIn copy: "AI made you faster. Then the treadmill sped up." (vertical GIF)

## Post (recommended)

AI made me faster. The treadmill sped up with me.

That's the part nobody puts in the demo. Research keeps finding that when AI makes work cheaper, we don't bank the time. We spend it.

- A UC Berkeley team (HBR) followed 40 people at a 200-person tech company from April to December 2025. Faster pace, wider scope of tasks, and work leaking into breaks and evenings.
- An Upwork survey of about 2,500 workers (2024): 77% said AI has added to their workload.
- It's the Jevons paradox. In 1865 more efficient steam engines meant more coal burned, not less.

The GIF is how it feels from the inside: the speedometer jumps, the tasks keep coming, and the finish line moves.

What I'm trying instead: decide what the saved time is for before the day starts, keep one real pause, and say no to scope that only exists because "AI can do it fast".

Has AI given you time back at work, or just raised the pace? 👇
