The AI Bubble Machine: Inside the $1 Trillion Circular Deal Web

OpenAI has reportedly committed to somewhere between $665 billion and more than $1 trillion of computing power while earning around $40 billion a year. The money flowing between Nvidia, OpenAI, Oracle, CoreWeave, AMD and the cloud giants runs in a circle. This video follows every arrow: who invests in whom, who borrows what, the accounting fight over GPU lifespans, the bull case, what the dot-com fiber bust really left behind, and what breaks if one link snaps.
