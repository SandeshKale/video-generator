## CHAPTER 05 — ONE DOLLAR, THREE LEDGERS  _(60s)_

Now, a big deal isn't automatically a suspicious one. Circularity is what makes people nervous.

When an investment helps a customer afford your product, your revenue goes up, your stock goes up, and that higher stock price funds your next investment.

The same dollar shows up as an investment on one balance sheet, as revenue on another, and as growth on a third.

It isn't necessarily fraud. But it's the same pattern that haunted the telecom boom, when companies sold capacity to each other and booked it as real demand.

And there's a twist. By early twenty twenty six, Nvidia's CEO said that hundred billion dollar figure was never a binding commitment. In March he said it was probably not in the cards, and Nvidia's actual stake in OpenAI's latest round was far smaller.
