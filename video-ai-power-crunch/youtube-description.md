**Description**
Everyone talks about AI chips. The real bottleneck is a transformer, a wire, and a power plant that doesn't exist yet. This video follows the electricity: Amazon's 20-year nuclear deal at Calvert Cliffs, Microsoft's Three Mile Island restart, the GE Vernova gas-turbine backlog, why PJM's capacity price went from $29 to $333 per megawatt-day, what it may mean for your bill, the $6.2B auction tied to data centers that don't exist yet, the House's 417–3 Ratepayer Protection Act vote, a Duke study on data-center flexibility, and Google's orbital TPU experiment.
