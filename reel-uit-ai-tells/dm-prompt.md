**Words that make text sound AI-written** (from Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" guide plus common chatbot habits):
delve · tapestry · testament · pivotal · crucial · underscore · showcase · intricate · vibrant · enduring · seamless · leverage · robust · navigate · landscape · realm · foster · elevate · embark · in today's fast-paced world

**Patterns that give it away:**
1. "It's not just X, it's Y" / "Not only… but also"
2. Everything in groups of three
3. Endings that sum up with nothing new ("In conclusion, X is a testament to…")
4. "Serves as" / "stands as" instead of plain "is"
5. Bold headers and bullet points for everything
6. Lots of em dashes —

**The fix: paste this after any AI draft:**
> Rewrite this like a busy human wrote it. Use plain, everyday words. Cut any sentence that adds nothing. No lists of three, no "not just X but Y", no big summary at the end. Keep my meaning and my facts exactly.

Honest note: none of these signs proves AI wrote something, and real people use these words too. They just make your writing sound generic.
