ChatGPT just stopped being a chat box. 👆

On Oct 7, OpenAI rolled out GPT-6 with something it calls **Intelligent UI**: instead of walls of text, ChatGPT can answer with a diagram you can poke, buttons you can tap, forms, charts you can edit, even a calculator it builds on the spot.

Here's what that looks like, scene by scene:

🛩️ Ask how a plane's wing makes lift and you don't get a paragraph. You get a diagram you can touch.
🧩 The model decides when words aren't enough, then builds the interface while it's still writing: buttons, forms, charts, a calculator.
🍛 OpenAI's own examples: a recipe that rescales when you change the guest count, a bill splitter, a map for a multi-day hike.
🔧 How it works, per OpenAI: a library of native, streamable UI components plus a compiler that assembles the interface as the answer is generated.
📅 Rollout: Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise (GPT-6 Sol) got it Wednesday. Free and Go (GPT-6 Luna) got it the next day. It's in the Chat tab only.
⚠️ The catch, as critics point out: a shiny chart isn't a checked chart. A pretty widget can make a wrong answer look more convincing. You can turn the visuals down in settings.

The real shift: an answer isn't something you read anymore. It's something you use.
