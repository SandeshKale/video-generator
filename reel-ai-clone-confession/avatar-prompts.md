# Avatar prompts — "I'm not him." (5 parts × ≤ 15 s)
**Built on `AVATAR-PROMPT-TEMPLATE.md`, with the guardrails from every earlier avatar reel kept:**
- tone first ("friend over coffee");
- identity lock;
- eyes always back on the lens;
- ≤ 2 nods, blinks every 3–5 s and none in the first second;
- no hand gestures;
- ≤ 1 stressed word per sentence;
- **no on-screen text or subtitles** (I still crop the host as a safety net);
- each prompt ~2.6k characters, well under the 4000 limit.

**What's different this time (the creative part):**
1. **Attire matches your photo, not a suit.** The premise is "clone vs original": your real profile photo appears as THE ORIGINAL polaroid next to the clone, so they should be wearing the same clothes. Framing, light and attire are locked across all five parts so the line-up of four clone copies reads as copies.
2. **Two performed beats, both kept small:**
   - Part 1: a ~0.5 s pause after "Actually" with a tiny head tilt (the hook's turn).
   - Part 3: the quoted line in a slightly hushed, hurried phone-call voice (no crying or shouting).
3. **One silence:** Part 5 holds about 1.5 s of closed-mouth silence, eyes on the lens, a faint knowing smile. This is the moment the clone "doesn't know the word". If the tool fills or skips it, that's fine: I'll build the silence in the edit.
4. **Plain black background** (the line-up wall, the call screen and the forensic close-up are all composited around it). If your tool insists on the bookshelf, it still works.

**How to run:** use the same photo for all five parts. **Generate Part 1 first and check:**
- Does "Hi, I'm Sandesh" look completely normal?
- Does the pause after "Actually" feel natural and not acted?
- Is the gaze on the lens throughout?

If yes, run the rest. If not, tell me what's off and I'll tighten before you spend credits on the other four.

**One question before Part 1:** is your avatar tool's voice **trained on your real voice**? If yes, I'll change Part 1 to "Same face, same voice. He typed this, I'm saying it." If not (a stock voice), keep the line as written. The reel must not claim "same voice" unless it's true.

**Pronunciation to check:** McAfee (MAK-a-fee), F-B-I, Papa, Ma. If the tool says "Mc-Afee" oddly, use "Mac-Afee" in the script line.
