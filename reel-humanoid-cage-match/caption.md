A content creator stepped into a cage in San Francisco and fought three humanoid robots: a small Unitree G1 first, then two modified T800s, with kicks up to 850 pounds, the company says. The clip went viral as "human vs. AI."

But every robot had a human pilot, driving it live with VR gear or a gamepad, its partner says. The AI's only job was balance: keeping the robot upright. The fighting itself is a video game with a body.

The pilots even qualified in a simulator: 3,670 matches. Then, roughly 8 million views later, California stepped in with a cease-and-desist, 12 days after the fight (Sept 18 → Sept 30).
