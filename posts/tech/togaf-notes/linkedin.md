## First comment (sources, caveats)

Exam facts were checked on 19 Oct 2026 against The Open Group's certification pages:
• Part 1 (Foundation): 40 multiple-choice questions, 60 minutes, pass 60% (24/40), closed book.
• Part 2 (Practitioner, OGEA-102): 8 scenario questions, gradient scored 5 / 3 / 1 / 0, 90 minutes, pass 24/40, open book (an electronic copy of the Body of Knowledge is built into the exam). Requires Foundation or a Part 1 pass on the same day at the same test centre.
• Combined exam (OGEA-103): 150 minutes.
https://certification.opengroup.org/node/713 · https://certification.opengroup.org/node/714

Caveats: these are revision notes, not the standard. Exam formats and pass marks can change, so confirm on The Open Group's site before you book. The ADM mnemonic and the "all second-best = 24" arithmetic are study aids, not exam strategy guarantees.
