## Caption
I was asleep when Claude wrote my morning brief. ☕ It reads my inbox and calendar and has it ready every weekday at 7 a.m. Here's the exact setup.

1️⃣ CONNECT Gmail and Google Calendar.
In Claude: Customize → Connectors → Discover → search "Gmail" → Connect to Claude → sign in with Google and approve. Do the same for Google Calendar. When it's done, both show as "Connected".

2️⃣ WRITE the brief like you'd brief a person. Paste this:
"Every weekday, brief me on:
1. today's meetings
2. emails that need me
3. anything I promised someone
3 sections, 5 lines each. Skip newsletters."

3️⃣ SCHEDULE it. Tell Claude: "Every weekday at 7 a.m., run my morning brief." Answer its questions, check the name, schedule and instructions it proposes, then hit Schedule. The frequency options are hourly, daily, weekly, on weekdays or manually.

It runs in the cloud, so it still runs when your laptop is asleep or the app is closed.

4️⃣ READ it with your coffee: Scheduled → Morning brief → latest run. Every run waits for you as its own session.

The clever part: add "Draft replies for anything that needs me. Don't send." Claude can write Gmail drafts, and sending still needs your explicit approval by default.

The catch:
⚠️ Scheduled tasks need a paid plan (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise).
⚠️ It only sees the one Google account you connected.
⚠️ It can't open attachments; it only sees file names and details.
⚠️ On Team and Enterprise accounts, an admin has to switch the connectors on first and sets the approval rules.
