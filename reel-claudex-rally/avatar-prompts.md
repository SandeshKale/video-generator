# Avatar prompts — "Nobody grades their own code." (5 parts × ≤ 15 s)

**Built on `AVATAR-PROMPT-TEMPLATE.md`, with every earlier guardrail kept:**
- tone first, identity lock, eyes always back on the lens;
- ≤ 2 nods, blinks every 3–5 s and none in the first second;
- no hand gestures, ≤ 1 stressed word per sentence;
- **no on-screen text or subtitles**;
- the same photo, framing, light and attire across all five parts.

**The hook (0–3 s):** Part 1 opens with a playful, curious question ("Would you let a student grade their own exam?"): brighter, a little quicker, a confident half-smile. After 3 s it settles into the usual calm.
