# Instagram caption — "Software is over?"

**On-reel title:** SOFTWARE IS OVER?

## Caption (recommended)
One developer got hit with one cancellation fee too many. So he rebuilt Photoshop. Then Premiere. Then five more Adobe-style apps, in a weekend, for free. 🧯

His name is Brandon Thomas, the project is called Artcraft, and it's seven open-source apps (PhotoCraft, FilmCraft, VectorCraft, LightCraft, EffectCraft, DesignCraft, PrintCraft) written in Rust with Claude Opus 5.5, under MIT / Apache-2.0 licences. The main repo already has 14,000+ GitHub stars. Free, local, no account.

The trigger: Adobe's early termination fee. Cancel an annual plan that you pay monthly and you can owe half of what's left. In March the US Justice Department proposed a $150 million settlement over how that was disclosed: $75M in civil penalties plus $75M in free services. Adobe denies wrongdoing, and the deal still needs a judge.

And the reality check: Thomas calls the apps a "super early alpha". He promised full parity in a month on Reddit, then told Hacker News it will take months. Users report basic bugs. So is software over? Not yet. But subscriptions just got a rival.
