# Next reel: Jatayu-style topics (after "Jatayu ud sakta tha?")

## What the Jatayu insights say (creator screenshots, about 12 h after posting)
| Metric | Value | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Views / viewers | 3,307 / 2,956 | best reel on the account (Pushpak 2,206 was the previous best) |
| Average watch time | 38 s of 100 s | long for this account; the story holds people who stay |
| Likes / comments / reposts / shares / saves | 92 / 2 / 1 / 3 / 5 | likes are fine; comments, shares and saves are weak |
| Comment rate | 0.1% ("lower") | the spoken "haan ya nahi?" prompt at 5 s did not work |
| Follows | 2 | about 1 follow per 1,500 viewers |
| Sources | Reels tab 87.4%, Explore 12.1% | almost all strangers, so the first seconds decide everything |

Retention curve, read off the graph (approximate):
- 0–3 s: falls to about 80%. Normal swipe-away.
- 3–10 s: falls to about 65%, during the question and the comment prompt.
- 10–35 s: slides from about 60% to about 42%. This is the real vulture intro and the first anatomy steps (feather, muscle).
- 35–80 s: almost flat at about 30–35%. Scaling, Argentavis and the vulture-loss story lose nobody.
- 80–100 s: drifts to about 22%, then drops to zero on the end card.

What to change next time:
1. Get to the first surprising fact by 8 s. The comment prompt cost a few seconds and earned 2 comments.
2. Cut the anatomy to two steps, not four. That stretch leaked the most viewers.
3. Aim for 70–80 s. The flat middle shows the story works; the opening and close are where length hurts.
4. Put the follow ask before the last payoff line, not after it. Everyone left on the end card.
5. Give people a reason to comment that is not an instruction: a guess they can be right or wrong about, answered at the end.
6. Give people a reason to share or save: something they can try or check themselves.

## The formula that worked
Film character or object the viewer just saw → the real animal, plant or place behind it → open it up with real photos → one honest limit ("the film size is hard") → a real, present-day twist that makes the viewer care (97% of vultures gone; the centre is named Jatayu).

Film facts used below: *Ramayana: Part 1* opened 5 Nov 2026 and ends on Jatayu's funeral; Hanuman appears only in a brief end scene; Part 2 is now reported for Diwali 2028. So Hanuman-side topics ride the "what comes next" curiosity, not a scene people have watched.

Still open from the earlier list: Konark's wheel-clock, the Delhi Iron Pillar, Gavi Gangadhareshwara for 14 January.

## Recommendation
**Sanjeevani first.** It is the only topic where the viewer can do the experiment, which targets the two weak numbers (comments and shares). **Jambavan's bear second**, if a film still or cast confirmation turns up, because it repeats the Jatayu arc most closely. **The fake Ashok third.**

Before scripting any of them: open the primary sources (the *Current Science* paper, the IUCN assessments, Wildlife SOS's own timeline), since everything above came from search summaries.
