# Topics tied directly to scenes in *Ramayana: Part 1* (after Jatayu)

## What is actually on screen in Part 1 (from reviews and an ending explainer, read 16 Nov)
Ravana's origin, penance and boon from Brahma; his golden court in Lanka; Tataka; Sita's swayamvar and Shiva's bow; the exile; Shurpanakha and her nose; Khar-Dushan; Kumbhkaran (his CG was criticised); the golden deer; Sita's abduction in the Pushpak; the jewellery Sita drops; Jatayu's fight and funeral (the most praised sequence); a post-credit scene where Hanuman takes flight. Not found in reviews: Ravana's ten heads, Kailash, Jambavan.
Already made: Pushpak, Jatayu.

## Recommendation
1. **Sone ki Lanka** next: a number everyone has a feeling for (gold), one bespoke picture (the 22 m cube beside a cricket pitch, then beside a palace), and Yash's golden court is one of the film's most talked-about looks.
2. **The nose surgery** second: strongest "India did this first" pride and share value, with a real 1794 engraving to show. Needs care: no gore, no jokes about the character, and the Sushruta link stated exactly as the sources allow.
3. **Kumbhkaran's sleep** third.
