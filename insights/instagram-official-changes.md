# What Instagram has officially changed (read 13 Oct 2026)

Only posts I could open on Instagram's own sites are marked **Official**. Everything else is third-party reporting and marked **Reported**.
Instagram has published **no new ranking-algorithm announcement since 30 Apr 2026**. The creators blog's latest posts (7 Oct, 30 Sep, 15 Sep, 8 Sep, 31 Aug) are about tools, plans and AI labels, not distribution.

## Official, and affects distribution
1. **30 Apr 2026, "Rewarding original creators on Instagram"** ([creators.instagram.com](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram)).
   - Accounts that mostly post unoriginal reels, photos or carousels are excluded from recommendation surfaces.
   - Borders, watermarks, subtitles and credits don't count as edits.
   - An account becomes eligible again when most of its recent posts are original over a rolling 30 days.
   - Check status under Account Status.
2. **31 Aug 2026, "Bringing More Transparency to AI-Generated Profiles"** ([creators.instagram.com](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/ai-generated-profile-label)).
   - The "AI creator" label is now the "AI-generated profile" label.
   - Instagram will "limit the reach of profiles that feature an AI-generated person and do not add this label".
   - Detected profiles are notified, and Account Status shows loss of recommendation eligibility.
   - Creators can add the label or appeal.
   - Creators who add it themselves "will not see a change to their profile's reach".
   - Creators who "simply use AI tools as part of their creative process" don't need the label.
   - The post does not mention AI avatars or AI voices.

## Official, tools only (nothing about ranking)
- 30 Sep 2026, **Edits assistant** ([edits.instagram.com](https://edits.instagram.com/blog/edits-assistant/)). It reads your own metrics (follows, views, retention, likes, shares) and suggests hooks, scripts and audio. US only, free with a daily limit.
- 15 Sep 2026, **Meta One creator plans** ($14.99–$499/month). The post says nothing about paying affecting reach. Only "optimized search" is mentioned ([creators.instagram.com](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/meta-one-creator-plans)).
- Instants (a Close-Friends-only photo feature) and shoppable Reels. Neither is a ranking change.

## Reported (third-party), not verified against Instagram
- **Signals:** watch time, sends per reach and likes per reach are the main ranking signals, named by Mosseri in Jan 2025. Sends are said to matter most for non-followers. The "3–5× weight" figure is only a community estimate.
- **"Your Algorithm":** users can see, add and remove their inferred topics and reset suggestions. It launched on Reels in Dec 2025, then Explore in Apr 2026, and reportedly the main feed in Jun 2026. The dates conflict between sources.
- **Hashtags:** a cap of 5 per post, reported by multiple outlets.
- **Reels length:** the maximum is reportedly 20 minutes, but Reels over 3 minutes reportedly aren't recommended to new audiences. I could not find this in Instagram's own help pages.
- **Trial Reels:** shows a reel to non-followers first. The follower minimum is unclear.
