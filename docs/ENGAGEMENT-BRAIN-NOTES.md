# Keeping the whole viewer engaged (14 Nov 2026)

Written after creator feedback on `reel-surya-tilak` v1 ("start too cold… people aren't moving… temple bland… research how to keep all parts of their brains engaged"). A working checklist, not a literature review. **Confidence labels:** [E] established research idea, named so it can be looked up; [P] popular-science claim only, treat as a hint. The two web searches run for this note returned mostly secondary articles, so the [E] items rest on the named work, not on pages opened here.

| System to engage | Idea | What it means for a reel | Surya Tilak v2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attention (orienting) | The orienting response: sudden onsets, cuts, motion and sound changes pull attention automatically (Lang's limited-capacity model) [E] | Frame 0 must already contain an event; pair each visual onset with a sound | Frame 0 = real sanctum photo with the beam on the forehead + bell hit; voice starts at 0.00 s |
| Curiosity | Information-gap theory (Loewenstein 1994): a stated gap you can almost close [E] | Open with a claim that needs explaining, then delay the answer | "Yeh tilak kisi insaan ne nahi lagaya. Ise sooraj ne lagaya hai!" |
| Reward / prediction | Surprise (better or different than expected) drives dopamine signalling [E in general, P for short video] | One reversal mid-reel; do not telegraph it | "Next year the same mirror missed" |
| Emotion and memory | Emotionally arousing moments are remembered better [E] | Use a real, culturally loved image at the emotional peaks | The real idol photo at the hook and at "sirf 58 mm" |
| Social brain | People watch people; visible action and gesture keep attention (mirror-system claims are debated) [E/P] | Figures must do things: walk, bow, raise hands, work | Queueing crowd, bowing devotees, aarti priest, engineers walking |
| Story | Narrative transportation (Green & Brock): attention + feeling + imagery together [E] | One cause chain, one object followed | The sunbeam from sky to forehead |
| Shared experience | Films that engage synchronise viewers' brain activity (Hasson's inter-subject correlation) [E] | Tight structure, clear cuts, unambiguous pictures | Real photo → model → photo |
| Learning | Mayer's multimedia principles: signalling, temporal contiguity (word and picture together), coherence [E] | Each object appears on its word; remove decoration that competes with the mechanism | Decoration is hidden in close-ups (garlands, petals only in wide shots) |
| Hearing | Music sets arousal and marks structure; silence is a cue | Audible bed (voice 15–19 dB above it), holes at the losses | Bed raised; holes on the miss and the cloud |
| Familiarity | Self-relevance: things from the viewer's own life hold attention | Everyday observations tied to the mechanism | Roof-hole sunbeam, mirror flash, lens and paper |

## Rules taken from this
1. Frame 0 is the most emotional real image available, with the key event already happening and a sound hit. Never open on an empty or dark establishing shot.
2. A real photograph at the start, at the emotional peak and wherever the script names a real thing; schematic 3D for the mechanism. Label which is which.
3. Every human figure has an action in every shot it appears in.
4. Sets are dressed (garlands, lamps, flags, carpet, carving), but dressing is removed from close-ups of the mechanism.
5. Music is audible: voice over music + effects median 15–19 dB, never below ~9 dB, with a limiter that guarantees it.
