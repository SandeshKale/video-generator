## 7. Content plan the profile must signal

Three pillars, one recognisable look each, same creator face and same tone (calm, specific, honest about limits).

| Pillar | What it covers | Series tag (the 5th hashtag) | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Science** | The body, space, everyday physics, "what's actually happening" | `#WhatsActuallyHappening` | Every number from a primary paper or official source, tier A, read on the day. Say "scientists disagree" when they do. |
| **Wellness** | Sleep, movement, food, stress, habits | proposed `#EvidenceCheck` **[NEEDS SANDESH to approve]** | **Evidence first.** No diagnosis, no cures, no supplement or product claims, no "doctors hate this". Always show the study size and species ("in mice", "in 41 adults"). Close with one line: "General information, not medical advice." |
| **Tech** | AI tools and how-tos, what a product really does | `#UseItToday` | Every command and number checked on the vendor's page the same day. Terminal output labelled "illustrative". Honest "catches" beat before the CTA. |

**Cadence:** three reels a week (Mon / Wed / Fri), about 30 s each, same file to YouTube Shorts. **Caption:** 300-700 characters, plain words, one emoji at most, sources named, disclosure lines, then exactly **5 hashtags** (topic, intent, audience, niche keyword, series). **First comment:** a question, not a promise.
Full method: `insights/caption-template.md`, `insights/hashtag-research.md`, and each reel's `caption.md`.
