## Post (recommended)

Everyone got the same AI. Only some teams lit up.

Gallup surveyed 43,262 US workers in 2026 and found something every leader rolling out AI should see. Follow the numbers in the GIF (one window = 1 in 100 employees, lit = engaged):

1. Manager doesn't back AI use: 30% engaged.
2. Manager actively backs it: 48% engaged.
3. Frequent use + a clear AI plan + a manager who backs it: 53%. The US average is 31%, and it hasn't moved since 2024.

What each lever is worth on its own, in points of engagement:
- Manager actively supports the team's AI use: +18 (48% vs 30%)
- A clear AI integration plan: +15
- Using AI at least weekly: +8

These are separate comparisons, so don't add them up. Gallup's line says it better than I can: "Making AI available is not the same as having a clear AI implementation plan."

One more number: among weekly AI users at AI-adopting companies, 9% gave AI's productivity impact the top score when their manager wasn't actively backing it, and 33% when they were.

Caveats: it's survey data, not proof of cause. Engaged people may also just be more likely to report a supportive manager. The three towers are subgroups, not three companies.

If you had to fix one of the three first: the plan, the habit or the manager?

## First comment (sources, how to read it, caveats)

Source: Gallup, "Employee Engagement Remains Flat as AI Adoption Accelerates", Jim Harter, 21 July 2026. I read the page in full. Surveys of Gallup Panel members (Feb 4-19 and May 6-20, 2026), 43,262 US adults working full or part time (±0.7 pts); AI-integrated subsample 21,724 (±1.0 pts).

How to read the GIF:
- Each window is 1 in 100 employees and a lit window is an engaged employee. The towers are subgroups from the report, not three companies: employees whose manager does not (30%) or does (48%) actively support their team's AI use, and the group with frequent use, a clear plan and manager support (53%).
- The lever bars (+18, +15, +8) are separate comparisons from the report (manager support 48% vs 30%; clear plan +15 pts; weekly-or-more use +8 pts within AI-adopting organisations). They are not additive.
- 31% US engagement, 18% actively disengaged, 36% peak in 2020: all from the same report. The "$2 trillion a year" is Gallup's own estimate of the cost of not-engaged and actively disengaged employees.
- The 9% → 33% productivity figure is the share of weekly-or-more AI users in AI-adopting organisations giving the top rating, without vs with active manager support.
- This is correlational survey data. Gallup does not claim causation on the page.

Disclosure: I used Claude to help analyse the report, draft this post and build the animation.
